**Hiccup** — one word for my Wi-Fi.

It never dies outright. It hiccups: a stutter in the middle of a call, a page that loads halfway and stops, a video that freezes for one beat and then carries on as if nothing happened. Everyone who has lived with home Wi-Fi knows that hiccup — small, repeated, always at the worst moment. It is the funniest and truest word for it because it is exactly what the connection does, several times a day, without ever apologising.
